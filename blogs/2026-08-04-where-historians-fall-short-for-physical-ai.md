Physical AI enables machines and industrial systems to perceive conditions, reason about them, and act in the real world. In industrial settings, as part of an industrial AI strategy, physical AI models can help organizations identify risks earlier, optimize operations, and respond to changing conditions in real-time. Delivering these outcomes starts with training AI models on detailed historical operational data.
