For teams moving to v3 from earlier versions of InfluxDB, query migration is often one of the last major hurdles. Explorer 1.9 introduces an AI-assisted Flux-to-SQL converter to help automate that process, while also bringing InfluxQL directly into Explorer. On top of that, this release adds two new live sample data simulators, an improved plugin log viewer, search across every list page, and query error history.
