Every month, satellite operators perform thousands of orbital maneuvers as recommended by LeoLabs’ data. They don’t do it to reposition satellites, they do it to avoid hitting things. With tens of thousands of active satellites and other debris circling the Earth, adding new devices to orbit comes with risk.
